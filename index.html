SDG 6 – Clean Water and Sanitation

SDG 6 focuses on ensuring availability and sustainable management of water and sanitation for all. My project supports this goal by helping users monitor hydration habits, learn about water conservation, and report local water-related issues through a mobile application.

Lab 2 – Interaction

Projects

Project 1 – Aglea

Problem Statement:
Many individuals lack a consistent way to monitor their daily water consumption, leading to chronic dehydration and a lack of awareness regarding personal water usage habits.

Description:
Aglea is a hydration monitoring application that helps users track daily water intake and maintain healthy hydration habits.

Reflection:
I learned how to integrate all previous lab knowledge into a complete mobile application. This project improved my problem-solving skills and helped me create an app that can be used personally without internet access.

Project 2 – Aglea

Problem Statement:
Many people are unaware of the specific UN targets for water conservation and lack a way to report local water infrastructure issues. My app solves this by integrating live SDG education, real-time weather-based hydration tips, and a community reporting portal.

Description:
An enhanced version of Aglea integrating cloud services, APIs, educational SDG content, and community reporting features.

Reflection:
I learned how to use Google Cloud, Firebase, and external APIs to create a more advanced application. This project strengthened my understanding of cloud-based mobile development and real-world integration.
